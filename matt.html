Transport reality
  • Tube and Elizabeth line are more reliable than taxis for central London daytime hops.
  • Canary Wharf, ExCeL, Royal Victoria and Greenwich legs usually favour DLR or Elizabeth line, not road.
  • Black cabs and rideshares can help on short awkward legs, but weekday traffic can kill precision.
  • Allow extra buffer for station exits, escalators, venue access, and security at conference sites.
Overall verdict
  • Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Monday are broadly workable with discipline.
  • Sunday is the ugliest day and may need one meeting shortened or moved.
  • Tuesday is busy but manageable if meetings actually start and end on time.
  • Wednesday is incomplete until the two unknown venues are confirmed.

Travel assumptions

  • Target is arrival around 5 minutes early.
  • Walking times are approximate and assume normal pace with conference gear.
  • Tube, DLR and Elizabeth line directions are shown where relevant.
  • Likely delays means normal London friction, not severe disruption.
  • Where an address or time is missing, the plan flags that instead of guessing hard.
