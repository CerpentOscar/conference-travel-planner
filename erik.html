Mixed
  • Friday is easy but partly blocked by the TBC lunch.

Best moves
  • Use Elizabeth line east/west whenever Canary Wharf is involved.
  • Walk in the City where possible.
  • Taxi is the cleanest fix for the late-night chaos.
Red flags
  • Tuesday 15:00–18:00 overlap.
  • Tuesday 18:00–20:00 chain.
  • Any TBC venue is a liability, shockingly.

Bottom line

  • These are practical planning estimates, not live TfL instructions.
  • Anything marked TBC or overlapping needs a final venue/time check.
  • Weekday traffic means taxi is often only useful for short awkward hops.

Check live transport and venue access before travel. London enjoys being annoying for sport.
