Mixed

Helpful patterns
  • Use the Tube for long central hops, taxi for awkward venue edges.
  • Stay on foot when the venues are clustered in the City / South Bank.
  • Do not pretend overlapping events are magically compatible.
Red flags
  • Saturday 18:00 and 19:30 clashes.
  • Sunday evening overload.
  • Tuesday after 18:30 needs a hard decision.

Bottom line

  • These are practical planning estimates, not live TfL instructions.
  • Anything marked TBC or overlapping needs a final venue/time check.
  • Weekday traffic means taxi is often only useful for short awkward hops.

Check live transport and venue access before travel. London enjoys being annoying for sport.
